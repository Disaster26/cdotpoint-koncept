C.point in a nutshell

We concentrate on delivering the highest quality consulting and communication services for public and private sector entities. Our aim is to pursue ambitious projects, which positively impact business environment and enhance people's quality of life.

Every case is different and needs a customised approach. As consultants we do not stop our work when the project is finished: we stay as close advisors, focused on discovering directions for further development.

Over 250 projects delivered for public and private clients in the last 20 years, including over 130 complex public sector projects.

What we offer

Each project requires a different approach. The general processes might be similar, but the key to success is to listen to and understand the real needs and fears of people and communities involved.

  1. Strategy & Innovation support

    A single, fundamental document: the vision of development and the road map to reach it, for cities, regions and private entities. Also innovation support systems, clusters, industrial parks and incubators.

    Projects: EC1SE, Granary Island, Masterplan

  2. Urban development & Investment support

    A second life for degraded areas, former factories, train stations and retail centres, and attracting new investments, connecting technical, environmental and socio-economic aspects.

    Projects: EXPO, Historical city centre, Science and Industrial Park

  3. Organisation & Transformation

    Diagnosis of dysfunctions and re-design of the organisation: PMO, a step-by-step methodology, better customer service and lower operating costs.

    Projects: Telecom operator, Development agency, Shipyard

  4. Reputation management

    Communication strategy audits, reputation risks management, crisis communication, M&A communication, local communities advisory and corporate responsibility planning.

    Projects: Retail chain, Energy provider, Multinational conglomerate

  5. Smart city & Mobility

    Maturity and interoperability of existing solutions, support for new implementations on the public side, and helping technology firms, developers and operators reach public sector clients.

    Projects: Smart City_HD, Intelligent transportation systems

  6. Good Governance & PPP

    Diagnosis, goals, resources and a project management plan, always with a risk and mitigation plan, KPIs and monitoring. Sharing investments, risks and profits between public and private bodies.

    Projects: Intelligent transportation systems, Shipyard, ITI

  7. Sustainability management

    An honest, step-by-step approach: for cities and regions working towards sustainable development goals, and for companies assessing and communicating their impact on the environment.

    Projects: Warsaw Metropolitan, Granary Island

  8. Internal communication

    Internal structures revision and planning, internal communication strategy, employer branding advisory, communication and presentation skills trainings.

    Projects: Consumer electronics manufacturer, Retail chain
